## 7. Decisions for you

1. Positioning. You said both: developers, builders and main contractors first, suitable residential kept. The enquiry data says the site currently brings in householders, more than half from outside the patch. Agreeing "both" means agreeing what we say no to. Proposal: keep the residential pages and form, stop bidding on residential terms outside Surrey, and rewrite demolition and earthworks for commercial buyers.
2. Commercial surfacing. Which business owns it. If SHS, the ad group and its landing page come out of the Surrey Contracting account. If Surrey Contracting, it goes on the main site openly and the SHS account stops bidding on car park terms. Today's arrangement, where the main site says no surfacing and a subdomain says the opposite, is the worst of both.
3. One definition of a lead. Form sent, or a call. Phone taps and started forms become secondary. Applies to both accounts so every report means the same thing.
4. Who owns what. The Google Ads account left my manager account on 9 September. The Business Profile should have you as primary owner and both agencies as managers, so it survives any change of supplier. Same for the Ads account.
