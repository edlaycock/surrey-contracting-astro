## Commercial surfacing FAQs

Talk to us early if access, phasing or drainage coordination need to shape the work.

Can work be phased around a live site?+

Yes. We can discuss access windows, route management and sequencing when preparing the project quotation.

Do you coordinate groundworks and drainage?+

Yes. Combined groundworks, drainage and surfacing packages help coordinate the build-up below and above the finished surface.

Which materials suit paths and public areas?+

The suitable choice depends on use, setting and maintenance needs. We work with gravel, Breedon gravel, resin, tarmac and paving.

Can you include kerbs and edgings?+

Yes. Kerbs and edgings can be included to define routes, vehicle areas and boundaries within the wider surfacing scope.
